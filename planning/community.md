# community

Services for dgg's community, built on a dgg login and on what the logger and
the [archives](archives.md) know about chat. Notes only, not yet planned: noted
2026-11-25.

## Services
- **Vouching:** connect a dgg account to a profile of links to the same
  person's accounts on other platforms, signed by the service, so others can
  check the link is theirs.
- **Pastebin and file uploads:** posts by community members.
  - Content moderators can delete posts.
  - Other users can add emote reactions or upvotes, which may be worth karma.
- **Betting ledger:** a record of bets between chatters and their terms:
  who bet whom, on what, for what stakes, and how it was settled.
- **Reputation:** a service of its own, which the others ask. It includes
  karma (see below).

## Uploads
Anything users upload needs:
1. A dgg login.
2. For file uploads, enough good reputation that the user is known to be a
   community member: not a bot, or a brand new or barely used account.

## Reputation
- Some flairs are worth reputation by default: broadcaster, moderator,
  subscribers, a history of donations, verified.
- So are awards: a month's top chatters, and chatter or orbiter of the year.

## Karma
- Earned from stats: top greentexters, and others like it, each worth some
  kind of karma.
- And from reactions and upvotes on posts.
- Perhaps several axes, not just good and bad: qualities, each scored, shown
  as a radar chart.
